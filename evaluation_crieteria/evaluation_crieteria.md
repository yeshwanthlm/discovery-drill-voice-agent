Scoring: Numeric
Max Score: 10

Scoring Rubric:
Score 10: Persona stayed fully in character throughout. Evaluator delivered a debrief with a specific verbatim quote from the trainee and one clear, actionable takeaway. Trainee had genuine back-and-forth — asked at least one follow-up question building on something the persona said. get_session_history was called near the start.
