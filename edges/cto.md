Label: the roleplay has reached a natural end and it's time for the debrief

LLM Condition: The discovery roleplay has clearly ended. This means the trainee has explicitly signalled they are finished (for example "I think that's all my questions", "let's wrap up", or a goodbye), OR the executive has explicitly ended the meeting due to their time limit. Do NOT take this edge just because the trainee asked a question about outcomes, success, or six-month goals — those are discovery questions, not wrap-up signals. Only route to the Evaluator when the trainee is genuinely done asking.
